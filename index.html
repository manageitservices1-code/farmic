Cold-pressed, the traditional way

Cold pressing extracts oil at low temperature and without chemical solvents, keeping more of the seed's natural vitamins, antioxidants and flavour intact than refined oils. It's slower and yields less oil per seed — which is exactly why the sourcing behind it matters.
