Our story

Farmic — straight from farm to customer

Most food travels through more hands than anyone realizes before it reaches a kitchen. Farmic exists to shorten that path.

Farmic is built on that idea.

Our name comes from what we do: straight from the FARM, through the hands that grow and mill it, connected by a direct line — that's the i, shown as the small arrow running under our logo — all the way to the Customer. No wholesalers. No retailers. Just the shortest path we could build.

Farmic exists to connect that chain directly — a single, trusted, transparent path from field to family.

We started with a simple promise: to carry pure, honest seeds and grains from the mill to the cold-press shops that need them. That led to the next step — our own outlets, close to the communities we serve, and the Farmic app that put ordering directly in customers' hands. Both are built. Now we're extending that path further still: partners can join us as a franchise, or call on our end-to-end consultancy to build a cold-press store of their own.

No link in that chain matters more than another. The farmer who sowed the seed. The miller who pressed it into purity. The customer who just wants to know their food is real. Farmic holds them all together — not as a supply chain, but as a direct relationship.

Straight from farm to customer. Nothing lost along the way.

What the mark means

Our logo carries the same story. The green-and-amber checkered pattern echoes a farm field and packaging cloth, framing a delivery truck at the centre. Around it, six small icons trace the journey a seed takes: from farmer, to miller, to distributor, to customer, and finally to food on the table — a cycle that feeds back into the name itself. And in the wordmark, a small arrow runs beneath "Farmic" — the most literal version of our story, drawn right into the name.
